KGF GYM

Gym management, simplified

KGF Gym is an Android app for gym owners and managers. It manages members, membership plans, payments and receipts, WhatsApp and SMS renewal reminders, expenses, revenue and profit reports.

  • Works offline. Data lives on the gym's phone.
  • Backups go to a private folder in the gym owner's own Google Drive, so data survives a lost or replaced phone.
  • No ads, no tracking, and no developer access to gym data.
