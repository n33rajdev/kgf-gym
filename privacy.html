KGF GYM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

KGF Gym is an Android app that a gym owner or manager uses to manage gym members, memberships, payments, expenses and reminders. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it is stored.

Who controls the data

All gym data entered in KGF Gym belongs to the gym owner who uses the app. The app's developer does not operate any server or database that stores this data and has no access to it.

Data stored on the phone

The app stores the following on the device where it is installed:

  • Member details entered by the gym manager: name, photo, date of birth, gender, phone numbers, email, address, emergency contact, membership plan and dates, trainer and notes.
  • Payments, receipts, expenses and expense receipt photos.
  • Reminder history and gym settings.

This data is kept in the app's private storage and excluded from Android's automatic device backups.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive

The gym owner signs in with their own Google account. KGF Gym receives the account's name, email address and profile picture to identify the owner inside the app.

With the owner's permission, KGF Gym uses the Google Drive app data folder (scope drive.appdata) to save backups of the gym data, so the data can be restored after reinstalling the app or on a new phone. This is a hidden folder in the owner's own Google Drive that only KGF Gym can access. KGF Gym cannot see, read or change any other files in the owner's Google Drive, and it does not access Gmail, contacts or any other Google service.

Backups stay in the owner's Google account until the owner deletes them (Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → KGF Gym → Delete hidden app data). Uninstalling the app does not delete them.

KGF Gym's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Messages to members

When the manager sends a reminder, KGF Gym opens WhatsApp, the SMS app or the phone dialer with the message filled in. The manager sends it from that app. KGF Gym does not send messages itself.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of data to anyone.
  • No use of data for any purpose other than running the gym-management features.

Deleting data

The manager can delete members and records in the app. To remove everything, uninstall the app and delete KGF Gym's hidden app data in Google Drive as described above. Members who want their data removed should contact their gym.
